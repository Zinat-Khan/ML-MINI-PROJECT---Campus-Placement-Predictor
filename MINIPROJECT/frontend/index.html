Data Privacy & Ethical Use Agreement

Welcome to the Campus Placement Predictor. Before analyzing your profile, please review how your information is used:

  • Advisory Guidance Only: This tool estimates placement likelihood based on past historical trends to help prioritize your preparation. It does not guarantee placement or company selection.
  • Data Privacy & Least Storage: Your inputs are processed locally or in-memory. No personal data is stored unless you explicitly opt in.
  • Protected Attributes Excluded: Demographics like gender, caste, or religion are never used to compute predictions.
  • Unmeasured Factors Notice: Static records cannot measure confidence, attitude, adaptability, or HR suitability—which recruiters evaluate heavily.

📜 Official Model Card & Data Sheet

Model Version: 2026.2.0-social (Calibrated Logistic Regression)

1. Model Details

Model Type: Calibrated Regularized Logistic Regression (Platt Sigmoid Scaling).

Data Range: Historical Institutional Batches 2023–2025 ($N = 600$).

Features Analyzed: Academic records, competitive coding, project quality, internships, GitHub activity graph, and LinkedIn profile completeness.

2. Intended Use & Non-Goals

Intended Use: Student self-guidance and preparation prioritization prior to campus placement drives.

Out-of-Scope Misuse: Candidate shortlisting, ranking, automated selection, or rejection decisions by recruiters or administration.

4. Stated Technical Limitations

  • Unmeasured Soft Attributes: Cannot measure interpersonal confidence, communication nuance, learning agility, or HR cultural fit.
  • Small Institutional Sample Size: Trained on historical data ($N = 600$ rows). Results are presented with an 80% bootstrap confidence interval.
  • Temporal Hiring Shifts: Corporate hiring bars shift annually based on macroeconomic conditions.
